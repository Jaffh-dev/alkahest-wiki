Simulate command generator

/alkdrops simulate rolls drops many times without killing or breaking anything. Its options must come in a fixed order; fill in the form and copy the command instead.

Enchantments

Using the result

  1. Copy the command (hover the box and press Copy) and paste it in chat, as an operator or with cheats on.
  2. The report shows each item's total, its average per run and the percent of runs it dropped in. Pity and one-time drops have their own lines.
  3. Long reports have pages: /alkdrops simulate page <n> shows another page without rolling again.

Notes

  • Simulations use their own fake player, so they never touch your real pity or first-drop progress.
  • With on periodic the number is ticks (20 per second), and rates are shown per minute.
  • Each simulated hit deals 1 damage, so damage_taken drops read "per damage".
  • Enchantments replace those levels on the held item (on the tool for blocks); level 0 removes one.

Every option is explained on the Commands page.
