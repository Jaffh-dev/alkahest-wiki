Alkahest Drops

Data-driven mob and block drops for Minecraft Forge. Datapacks and mods add, remove and tune drops with JSON files, checked when they load, simulated in game and shown in JEI.

Who are you?

Datapack guide I make datapacks or modpacks Write drop and removal files, set chances and roll counts, add enchantment bonuses, bad-luck protection and triggers, and test everything with /alkdrops. What it does

  • Add drops to any mob or block: chance, count, roll-based counts, NBT, vanilla loot conditions and functions.
  • Remove drops from the loot table, other mods and other datapacks, by item, tag, everything or experience.
  • Target one id, a tag, every mob or block of a mod, or everything.
  • Enchantment bonuses for any enchantment, not only Looting and Fortune.
  • Bad-luck protection, first-kill drops and per-player drops.
  • Triggers: death, shear, hit, lightning, and drops over time.
  • Drop actions: make a drop glow or run a datapack function when it lands.
